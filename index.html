How to use the SuperBuy spreadsheet

  1. Open a CAFANS listing and record the seller link, exact color, size and current item price. Confirm whether SuperBuy accepts the listing before paying.
  2. Compare the seller’s size chart with an item you already own. For shoes, check the stated foot-length range. For tees and hoodies, compare chest width and body length. For jackets, allow room for layers.
  3. If warehouse quality-check photos are available, match the variant and ask for a close-up of any unclear detail. Check shoe size labels, embroidery placement and jacket fasteners.
  4. Compare shipping quotes after you have the parcel’s weight and dimensions. Check the shipping line’s billable weight, restrictions and any fees before confirming dispatch.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shortlist of product listings used to compare items before ordering through a shopping agent. This page uses selected CAFANS listings for its product directory.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The table shows estimated item prices in USD. Get a separate international shipping quote for the destination and parcel, then include any applicable fees in your budget.

What if a listing changes or sells out?

Check the current seller listing and your selected variant. If you choose a replacement listing, check its size chart and dispatch estimate again before ordering.
